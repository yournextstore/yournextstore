# Notice

This theme ports the visual design of **Horizon**, the Next.js storefront starter by Swell
(https://github.com/swellstores/horizon), onto the Your Next Store storefront: the deep-indigo,
plum, sand and butter palette, Poppins throughout, the italic announcement bar, the centred
wordmark header, the full-bleed portrait hero with its indigo CTA and barred link, the rounded
portrait category cards, the two-weight statement band, the "Caring for you" panel, the white
cards on sand, the listing with its feature row and sidebar, the half-page product photograph,
the purchase row and counter, the bag drawer with its dashed "Add more products" tile, the
About page's overlapping colour blocks and line-drawn pillars, and the two-column footer.

Horizon's quiz, membership, subscription and testimonial flows are not ported: Your Next Store
has no data behind them. The testimonial band carries the shop's promises instead of reviews.
All copy is rewritten for a skincare and body care store; the Horizon name and logo are not used. The store
name and catalog come from the Your Next Store API.

## Images

No photographs from the original are shipped. Horizon's editorial photos are replaced, slot for slot,
by generated photographs (see "Generated photographs" below).
Product cards, product pages and the bag show the store's own product images from the Your Next Store API.

Only three line icons come from the original repository (`public/images/about-us/`):
`icon-curated.svg`, `icon-tested.svg`, `icon-plant.svg` (from `quiz.svg`, `research.svg`, `plant-base.svg`).
