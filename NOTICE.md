# Notice

The visual design of this storefront theme is a port of **Maison** by njX
(<https://github.com/njbSaab/astro-njx-maison>, <https://njxui.dev>), an Astro + Tailwind
CSS theme released under the MIT License. Its layout, type system (Playfair Display, Karla,
Cousine), paper/ink/bordeaux palette, archive facet rail, printed drawers and record layout were
re-implemented here on Next.js and the Your Next Store Commerce Kit.

No code, photographs, brand names or copy from the original are shipped: the original's demo
"houses", its watchmaker names and its photography are replaced by the store's own catalogue
data and images, and by editorial photographs made for this store.
