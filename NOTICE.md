# Notice

This theme ("009 · Enterprise Commerce") is a port of the storefront design of
[Blazity/enterprise-commerce](https://github.com/Blazity/enterprise-commerce) (live demo:
<https://next-enterprise-commerce.vercel.app>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below:

- the visual design: slate palette, Inter type, header with mega menu and black announcement bar, the
  hero carousel with its floating product card, the New Arrivals grid, the Featured Categories tiles,
  the product tile, the listing layout (sticky title and facets column, "Sort by", pagination), the
  product page (thumbnail column, accordions, similar products row), the cart drawer and the footer;
- the line icons in `components/icons.tsx` (bag, chevron, search, plus) and the social icons in
  `app/footer.tsx`.

No images, logos or brand names from the original are used: product photographs come from the
store's own catalogue, editorial photographs (hero slides, category tiles, about page) are generated photographs
(below), and the store name comes from the Store API.
