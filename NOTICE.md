# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **Skateshop** by Sadman Sakib (sadmann7),
https://github.com/sadmann7/skateshop, onto the Your Next Store storefront.

What was carried over: the shadcn/ui zinc palette (dark by default, with a light/dark toggle), the
Geist + Cal Sans type pairing, the sticky header with "Lobby"/category dropdown menus, the
"Search products… Ctrl K" bar, the outline cart button with a count badge, the lobby home page
(centred headline with a pill badge, "Buy now" + outline secondary button, category tiles, the
"Featured products" content section and the card grid that replaced "Featured stores"), the
bordered product card with "Add to cart" and "Preview" buttons, the product page (image carousel
with thumbnail strip, quantity stepper, "Buy now" / "Add to cart", collapsible description, a
scrolling "More products from …" row), the listing header with "Filter" sheet and "Sort" menu, the
cart sheet and the multi-column footer with the inline newsletter field.

What was left out: the multi-store marketplace, seller dashboard, authentication, subscriptions,
blog/MDX content and the "Build a board" page. Shopper accounts, checkout and payments are served by
the platform. No images from the original repository are used (its category and newsletter photos
are not covered by a licence we could verify); all product imagery comes from the store's own
catalogue. The original's brand name and copy are not used.

The skateboard logo glyph is the Tabler Icons "skateboard" icon (MIT, © Paweł Kuna), as used by the
original. Cal Sans is served from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
