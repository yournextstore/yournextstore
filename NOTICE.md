# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of **Ninth Wave** by shellcat-com / Parable
(https://github.com/shellcat-com/ninth-wave-surf), a static HTML/CSS/JS surf-apparel landing page,
onto the Your Next Store storefront. Its design system (sand, foam, slate, denim and coral palette;
Bebas Neue, Karla and Space Mono; the faded-poster hero, rotating stamp badge, surf-conditions
report grid, drifting product shelf, outlined watermarks, ink drop band and stats band) is reused
here and extended to the listing, product, cart and about pages the original does not have.

The original's brand name, copy and product illustrations are not used. The hero photograph
(`public/images/poster.jpg`) and the About page photograph (`public/images/pexels/surf-rack.jpg`)
are AI-generated for the Brinewash surf apparel demo store. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts via
`next/font` (SIL Open Font License).
