# Notice

This theme ("003 · Scentique") is a port of the **Scentique** storefront design from
[Gladiarn/Scentique-Monorepo](https://github.com/Gladiarn/Scentique-Monorepo) (the `frontend/` app;
live demo: <https://frontend-two-dusky-65.vercel.app>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below:

- the visual design: the espresso/champagne palette and scent-family tones, the Prata / Bodoni Moda
  italic / Hanken Grotesk type pairing, the plain-glass panels, the floating header with dropdowns,
  the hero with its featured-scent card, the story, families and best-sellers bentos, the waving
  closing banner, the scent card, the product page and footer compositions;
- the wave-path drawing in `components/brand/wave-backdrop.tsx` and the hairline icons in
  `components/icons.tsx`;
- the dark drape backdrop `public/images/drape-backdrop.webp` (from `frontend/public/images/hero`).

The raw-material still lifes in `public/images/materials/` (cedar, iris, amber, smoke) were generated for this
theme with an image model (FLUX.2 [pro]) and are not photographs of any real product or brand.

Product, collection and category images come from the connected store.

The Scentique name, logo and its labelled bottle photography are not used.
