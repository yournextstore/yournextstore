This storefront's design is a port of the free starter edition of **ASoc Bumble** by ASoc
(https://github.com/ASocTemplates/asoc-bumble-shop-html), released under the MIT License. Only the
three MIT pages of that repository (home, shop and product) were used as the reference: the cream,
pine and terracotta palette with its dark-mode twin, the Fraunces/Cabin type pairing, the announcement
bar, the two-row centred header, the hero slider, the circular category thumbnails, the product tile
with its hover "Add to cart" bar, the "Made to match" card, the photo strip, the shop sidebar filters,
the product page layout and the newsletter-band footer. The cart drawer, collection and about pages
extend that visual language; nothing was taken from the paid full edition.

No images, brand name or logo from the original are shipped: the ASoc Bumble name and bear roundel are
replaced by the store's own name and initial (the original hotlinks third-party stock photographs, which its MIT

catalogue photos. Fraunces and Cabin are served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.
