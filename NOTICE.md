# Notice

This theme ("030 · Hydrogen fashion theme") is a port of the storefront design of
[ozgursagiroglu/shopify-hydrogen-fashion-theme](https://github.com/ozgursagiroglu/shopify-hydrogen-fashion-theme),
a Shopify Hydrogen theme, onto Your Next Store. It has no public demo; the port follows the README
screenshots and the source.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design (the warm off-white / deep
black palette with its gold accent, the Cormorant Garamond + Plus Jakarta Sans pairing, the type and
radius scale, the announcement bar, the header with the centred wordmark and the full-width mega menu,
the near-black footer; the home sections: full-bleed hero, product carousel, "Shop by Category" tiles,
feature strip, the "As featured in" row, recommended grid, lookbook, newsletter band, split panels and
the photo strip; the collection banner, filter sidebar and sort, the product page gallery, selectors
and accordion, the cart drawer and the About page compositions).

Not taken: the "ada ÉLAN" brand name and logo, the demo photography (Unsplash seed content, not part of
the repository) and the demo copy that refers to the brand, its history, press mentions, testimonials
and store locations. The theme shows the store's own name, product photography and categories instead;
the press row lists the store's categories and the testimonials are omitted. Cormorant Garamond and
Plus Jakarta Sans are served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.
