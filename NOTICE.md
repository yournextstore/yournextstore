# Notice

This theme ports the visual design of **TeaVibe** by Yuriy Paray (https://github.com/yurapa/tea),
released under the MIT License, onto the Your Next Store storefront.

Ported: the cream / deep green / gold palette and its dark variant, Playfair Display + Inter
typography, the header (menu drawer, category search bar, theme pill), the hero carousel, product
cards, the "Special Deal" band (re-written as a catalogue-count band), benefit row, footer, listing
sidebar, product page buy box and cart summary.

The original's brand name, logo, copy, photographs and product photography are not used. Product cards,
product pages, the hero's product slide and the cart show the store's own catalogue photos. The editorial
photographs in `public/images/pexels/` are generated for this store (see "Generated photographs").
