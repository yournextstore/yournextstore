# Notice

This theme's visual design is a port of **Jamstack ECommerce Next** by jamstack-cms
(https://github.com/jamstack-cms/jamstack-ecommerce), used under the MIT licence below:
the powder-blue hero with its white disc, the light-grey product stages, the "Trending Now"
tiles, the outlined black button, the hairline quantity picker and the plain wrapping nav.

Not carried over:

- The original's product photography (retail furniture packshots) and its "JSEC" logo: the
  theme shows the store's own product images and a text wordmark built from the store name.
- The Eina 01 typeface (a commercial font bundled in the original repo): replaced by Figtree
  (SIL Open Font License), loaded through `next/font/google`.

All code and copy here were rewritten for this storefront; no files were copied verbatim.
