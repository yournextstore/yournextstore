# Notice

This theme ("006 · Naturelle") is a port of the **Naturelle** Shopify Hydrogen theme from
[Weaverse/naturelle](https://github.com/Weaverse/naturelle) (live demo:
<https://naturelle-shopify-hydrogen.weaverse.dev>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below:

- the visual design: the theme-setting palette (plum announcement bar and newsletter band, blush
  footer, mint ticker, sage story panel, lavender help band, warm greys), the Montserrat / Belleza
  type pairing, the two-row header with centred wordmark and menu, the home-page section order and
  compositions (image hero with the ellipse call to action, mission ticker, "Trending now" tiles,
  story slider, value cards, new-arrival split, best-seller spotlight, promo banners, product
  carousel, FAQ band), the product card, the product page, the collection banner, the cart drawer
  and the mobile menu;
- the hand-drawn ellipse path in `components/icons.tsx` (`EllipseIcon`).

The Naturelle name, logo, and the demo's photography (served from the demo's Shopify store, not part
of the repository) are not used. Product imagery comes from the store's own catalogue; editorial
slots use free Pexels photographs (hero, split banner, help band, about page) or generated photographs
(story, collection banners), both listed below; the sprig
mark and copy are original to this port.
