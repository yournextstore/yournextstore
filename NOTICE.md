# Notice

This theme ("001 · Sofa Society") is a port of the **Sofa Society** storefront design from
[Agilo/fashion-starter](https://github.com/Agilo/fashion-starter) (live demo:
<https://fashion-starter.agilo.com>, Figma: <https://www.figma.com/community/file/1494273775050024009>)
onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below:

- the visual design: layout grid, type scale, palette, header, footer, home, listing, product, cart and
  about page compositions;
- the line icons in `components/icons.tsx`.

## Photographs

The interior photographs in `public/images/interiors/` were generated for this template with an image model
(Black Forest Labs FLUX.2 [pro] through the Vercel AI Gateway). They show no real products, people or brands and
carry no third-party licence. Product, category and collection images come from the connected store.

## Original design

The Sofa Society storefront is itself derived from the Medusa Next.js starter, so both copyright notices
apply.
