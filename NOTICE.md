# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **UIkit E-commerce Template** by Roman Chekurov
(https://github.com/chekromul/uikit-ecommerce-template), released under the MIT License (below).

What was carried over: the layout system (dark toolbar, 60px white navbar with a mega "Catalog" drop,
off-canvas mobile menu and cart), the UIkit palette and type scale (Roboto, #f8f8f8 page, white cards,
4px radius, blue primary), the product card, the catalog page with its sidebar card and sort bar, the
product page with its tinted price panel, and the home page structure (slideshow, catalog row,
trending grid, about/latest pair, subscribe band, advantages row).

Not carried over: the original's product photographs, brand logos and product names (Apple, Dell,
Lenovo and others are trademarks of their owners), its category icons (Noun Project, separate
licence), and its placeholder copy. All products, prices and product images come from the store's
own data.

The home slideshow photographs in `public/images/pexels/` were generated for this store (see
"Generated photographs" below).

Colours were darkened where needed to meet WCAG AA contrast.
