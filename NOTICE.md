This storefront's design is a port of **Deckle** by Christian Chiavelli
(https://github.com/christianchiavelli/deckle), released under the MIT License. Ported: the
"Copper plate" token set (rag paper, bistre ink and copper palette, light and dark), the Host Grotesk
type roles, the announcement bar, header, ink footer, full-width bands, the print stage with proof
trim marks, print tiles, filter chips, size-option tiles, the copper feature band, the museum-record
detail list, the cart lines and summary, and the numbered steps.
