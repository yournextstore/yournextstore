# Notice

This theme ("028 · astro-njx-store") is a port of the **njx-store** storefront design from
[njbSaab/astro-njx-store](https://github.com/njbSaab/astro-njx-store) (live demo:
<https://astro-njx-store.pages.dev>), a free Astro + Tailwind theme, onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below:

- the visual design: paper/ink/pine palette, Bricolage Grotesque + Inter pairing, the floating card
  header and footer, announcement bar, home sections (hero, collection shelf, product grid, values
  card, pull quote, pine feature card), collection catalogue with chips and sort, product page,
  floating cart and menu drawers, about page composition and the light/dark switch;
- the 24px stroke icons in `components/njx/icons.tsx`.

Not taken: the original's name, logo and copy, and its photography (product and lifestyle images
come from the store's own catalogue).
