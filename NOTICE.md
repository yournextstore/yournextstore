# Notice

This theme ports the visual design of **Farside** by shellcat-com / Formwork
(https://github.com/shellcat-com/formwork-farside), a single-page expedition-gear landing page,
onto the Your Next Store storefront: the cold near-black field-manual palette with one signal-orange
accent, Anton / Chivo / Space Mono type, grid-reference eyebrows with scramble-in text, the
full-bleed hero with its canvas weather engine (snow, dust, driven rain), the segmented control with
a line-art diagram and spec readout, the numbered six-step walkthrough rail, the flat tier cards with
hatch "safety tape" details, the angled cut sections, the plan grid and the footer. The original has
no shop pages; its look is extended here to the listing, collection, product, cart drawer, menu and
about pages.

Shipped from the original under its MIT licence: the weather-engine code and the diagram styling
(ported to React; the tent drawings were replaced by new weather drawings). None of the original's
photographs are shipped. All copy is rewritten for an expedition and camping gear store; the store name and catalogue
come from the Your Next Store API.
