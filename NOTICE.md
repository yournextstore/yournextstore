# Notice

This theme ("005 · Demo Ecommerce") is a port of **AKVA**, the Hydrogen + Sanity demo storefront from
[sanity-io/demo-ecommerce](https://github.com/sanity-io/demo-ecommerce) (Remix / Shopify Hydrogen; storefront in
`apps/storefront`, live demo: <https://hydrogen-sanity-demo.com>), onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below (the repository's root `LICENSE`):

- the visual design: the off-black / peach / light-grey palette, the DM Sans type scale (12–110px, tracked
  -0.03em), the radii, the fixed transparent header that frosts white on scroll with a centred wordmark and a
  circular cart count, the "Products" side panel (pastel collection tiles and product pills), the full-screen
  mobile menu, the peach home hero with a product photograph and tile, the staggered two-column module grid
  (product cards, call-to-action, callout, shape-masked collection modules), the collection hero in a per-page
  colour theme, the full-height product stage with a sticky floating widget, the tinted related-products band,
  the cart drawer and the peach footer card.

Not taken: the "AKVA" name and sun logo, the Sanity / Shopify promotional footer, the demo's product photography
(served from Sanity's CDN, not part of the repository), the collection vector artwork (replaced by simple shapes
drawn for this theme), and all copy naming the original brand. The wordmark and every store name shown come from
the store's own settings; product and collection photographs come from the store's catalogue, and the
editorial photographs (home call-to-action, about page) were generated for this template (below).
