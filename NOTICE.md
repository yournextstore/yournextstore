# Notice

This theme's design is a port of **Sanity Market** (https://github.com/sanity-io/swag-store,
live at https://sanity.market), the Shopify Hydrogen + Sanity swag store by Sanity, released
under the MIT licence reproduced below.

What was taken: the layout and visual language (colour-block product grid, the right-hand cart
panel with labelled buckets over a halftone field, the bottom bar with brand blocks, navigation and
the black Total / Checkout strip, the giant category filter line, the catalogue / list switch, the
three-column product page with the green Add to cart bar, the information page and the sky-blue
footer), the palette and the pixel type scale.

What was not taken: no Sanity or third-party names, logos, wordmarks, squiggle mark or merchandise
photography, and no code from the original's Shopify/Sanity data layer. The original's commercial
display face (Waldenburg) is replaced by Inter Tight; IBM Plex Mono is loaded from Google Fonts.
The asterisk mark and all copy are original to this theme; products, categories and photos come
from the store's own catalogue.
