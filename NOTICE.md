# Notice

This theme ("022 · Next E-commerce Shopco") is a port of
[mohammadoftadeh/next-ecommerce-shopco](https://github.com/mohammadoftadeh/next-ecommerce-shopco)
(Next.js 14 storefront; live demo: <https://next-ecommerce-shopco.vercel.app>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the layout and component styling — the black
announcement strip, the header with its "Shop" menu and wide grey search pill, the grey hero band with a
wide uppercase headline, stat row and spinning four-point stars, the black band of names under it, the
centred "New arrivals" / "Top picks" product rows with "View All" pills, the rounded grey "Browse by"
bento, the bordered testimonial-style cards with arrows, the black newsletter card overlapping the grey
footer, the shop page's bordered filter card, "Showing … Products · Sort by" toolbar and
Previous/Next pagination, the product page's thumbnail column, 40px uppercase title, colour and size
pills, quantity pill with a black "Add to Cart" (pinned to the bottom on phones) and its
"Product Details · Rating & Reviews · FAQs" tabs, and the cart's "Order Summary" with promo field and
"Go to Checkout" button (shown here in the cart drawer).

No code was copied verbatim, and no images, logos, icons, fonts or brand names: the components are
written for this storefront and its data, the stars are redrawn as SVG, the original's model photography
is replaced by Pexels photographs (credited below), the brand logos in its black band (Versace, Zara,
Gucci, Prada, Calvin Klein) by the store's own category names, its invented customer reviews and "200+ brands" style
figures are replaced by real reviews (when the store collects them) or plain store promises and real
catalogue counts, and the "SHOP.CO" name does not appear. The original's fonts are not used: Integral CF
(Fontfabric, commercial) is replaced by Archivo (SIL Open Font License) at its widest, heaviest setting,
and Satoshi (Indian Type Foundry) by Figtree (SIL Open Font License).
