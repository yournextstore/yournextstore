# Notice

This storefront's design is a port of **Quiet Bar Theme** by Larry Xue (larry-xue),
https://github.com/larry-xue/quiet-bar — a vintage/industrial bar and pub website template built with
Astro. Its palette (deep charcoal, aged brass, terracotta, warm cream), type pairing (Playfair Display
over Source Sans 3), noise film, plus-sign masthead tile, letter-spaced section titles with the brass
bar, ornamental dividers, info bar, menu accordion cards, gallery treatment, press quote cards, contact
cards, "Please note" box, footer and 404 page were rebuilt here as React components over the Your Next
Store storefront, and extended to the listing, category, collection, product, cart and about pages the
original does not have.

No images from the original are used (its gallery ships only SVG placeholders). Product photographs
come from the store's own catalogue; the gallery, story frame and collection backdrop are AI-generated
store photographs (listed under Generated photographs below). The original's bar name, logo, menu, address, press quotes and
articles are not used; store, product, category and collection data come from the store's API.
