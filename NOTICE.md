# Notice

This storefront theme ports the "Chromatic" design of **ecommerce-template** by Marcos Penelas
Cámara (https://github.com/MarcosCamara01/ecommerce-template) onto the Your Next Store
storefront: the cool grey and night grounds, condensed Archivo display type, the floating glass
navigation pill, the home hero carousel (rising word, fanned photo deck, rotation ring), the
inking section rows, product tiles with the rising second photo and glass quick add, the giant
listing headings, the product page split, the bag drawer and the wordmark footer.

Parts of the original's code are adapted under its MIT licence: the hero rotation hook, the
photo deck and the rotation toggle (`components/home/`), the inking section row
(`components/home/section-row.tsx`) and the display-type width table (`lib/display-type.ts`).

No images, logos or brand name from the original or its demo are shipped. The "STORE" wordmark
is replaced by the connected store's name; every product and section, and every product
photograph, comes from the store through the Commerce Kit SDK. Its own auth, Stripe and Supabase flows are not ported: the
platform's checkout and account handle those.
