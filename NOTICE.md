# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **Next Bookstore** by Sat Naing
(https://github.com/satnaing/next-bookstore), released under the MIT License (reproduced below).

What was taken: the layout and visual language (Fraunces + Quicksand type pairing, the mint/"skin"
palette, top bar and header with Category menu, hero composition, book-row cards with muted image
panels, header cart dropdown, product details layout, about page and footer structure).

What was not taken: no images, book covers, book titles, logos or brand copy from the original are
shipped. Product photography comes from the store's own catalogue; the "book cover" look is a CSS
treatment of those photos. All copy was rewritten.
