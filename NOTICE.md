# Notice

This storefront theme ports the visual design of **Lazora** by Soni Canra Wiguna
(https://github.com/soni-canra-wiguna/lazora), released under the MIT License, onto the
Your Next Store storefront.

What was taken: the layout and visual language (grey top bar and white header with a pill
search, pastel banners with a serif display title and a pill button, grey shop tiles
with hover-in pictures, the product grid and card, the filter sidebar, the product page with a
thumbnail column and description panel, the cart drawer and the grey footer with a news
signup). The code was rewritten for this storefront; no images, logos or brand names from
Lazora are included. The original's display face (Canela, a commercial typeface) is replaced
by Newsreader (SIL Open Font License); body text is Plus Jakarta Sans (SIL Open Font License),
as in the original.
