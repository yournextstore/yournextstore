# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of **Grofresh** by Alexander Kolesnik (sobol-sudo),
https://github.com/sobol-sudo/grofresh, released under the MIT License (reproduced below).

What was taken from the original: the visual design (the 640px one-column app frame, the
Plus Jakarta Sans type scale, the flash-white / leaf-green palette, the soft card halo, the
"Fresh Deals" banner, round category chips, the product tile with its green cart-plus button,
the round counter, the add-to-cart / buy-now pill pair, the cart screen and the pill tab bar)
and the tab-bar and cart-plus icon shapes.

What was not taken: none of the original's images (product, category, banner, empty-cart and
avatar artwork) ship with this theme. Product and category imagery comes from the store's own
catalogue; the empty-basket illustration was drawn for this theme.

The round photos on the promo card (`public/images/pexels/promo-flatlay.jpg`, `promo-tote.jpg`)
are AI-generated for this theme's Pepperlark grocer demo store.
