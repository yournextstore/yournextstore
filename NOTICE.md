# Notice

This storefront theme ports the design of **forma-commerce** by SakuraUltra
(https://github.com/SakuraUltra/forma-commerce), a boutique fashion storefront for Next.js, onto the
Your Next Store storefront: the warm ivory and deep olive palette (with its dark mode), the
Georgia-style serif display type over Geist, the split hero with category links, the "good things,
on repeat" product row, the half-photograph "in focus" story panel, the sale edit, the trust strip,
the olive footer, the bordered filter toolbar, the product page with its lightbox gallery, the ⌘K
search panel and the cart drawer.

No original brand name, logo, copy or imagery is shipped: the original's photographs come from
Unsplash and are not part of this theme. Product photography, store names, products and categories
come from the Your Next Store API. The display face is Gelasio (SIL Open Font License), a
metric-compatible stand-in for Georgia.
