# Notice

This storefront theme ports the design of **astro-njx-dark-lux** by njbSaab (njX,
https://github.com/njbSaab/astro-njx-dark-lux), a monochrome dark-luxury Astro coffee theme, onto
the Your Next Store storefront: the near-black paper with white ink, Jost wide-tracked uppercase
type, zero radius and hairline borders that light up on hover, the full-bleed moody hero with the
offset three-column category mosaic, the photo-card product shelves, the white services band, the
editorial banner, white content pages, the fullscreen menu, the search overlay and the cart as a
centered white card over a dark blurred backdrop.

The original's brand name, logo, "star the repo" promo bar, theme-promotion copy and tech-stack
logos are not shipped; store names, products, categories and collections come from the Your Next
Store API.
