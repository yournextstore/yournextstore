# Notice

This theme ports the storefront design of **Attar** (nadidazwad/nextjs-perfume-store,
https://github.com/nadidazwad/nextjs-perfume-store) onto the Your Next Store storefront:
its design tokens (sage-and-forest palette, Geist type scale, radii), the stylesheet in
`app/storefront.css` (adapted from its `storefront.css`, `commerce.css` and `app-shell.css`),
and the layout of its header, mega menu, homepage sections, product card, listing, product
page, bag drawer and phone tab bar. Its admin area, store data, brand names and product
artwork are not used; all products, images and copy come from the store's own catalogue.
