# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of **Grilli**, a restaurant website template by codewithsadee
(https://github.com/codewithsadee/grilli), adapted into a lifestyle shop. Its layout, palette,
typography (Forum and DM Sans), section designs and interactions are reproduced here, and these
decorative files are taken from it unchanged: `public/images/separator.svg`,
`public/images/img-pattern.svg`, `public/images/footer-form-pattern.svg`,
`public/images/footer-form-bg.png` and `public/images/form-pattern.png`.

The original's food and people photographs, logo and icons are not included. Product cards, product
pages and the cart show the store's own images; the editorial slots use the generated
photographs listed below.
