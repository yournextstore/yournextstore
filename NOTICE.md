# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **ChopChop**, the Commerce.js demo store
(https://github.com/chec/commercejs-chopchop-demo), onto the Your Next Store storefront.

What was carried over: the split-screen layout (logotype and an italic "Shop:" index pinned on
the left, tall product tiles on the right), the comma-separated text navigation with the bag
count, the soft-cornered photographs that square off on hover, the per-product accent buttons,
the full-screen ecru shopping bag, the "Some other things you might like" row, the footer, and
the palette (ink #150703, ecru #FAF8F3, white rock #E8E0CF, clementine, tumbleweed, hawkes blue,
asparagus, goldenrod) with Inter and EB Garamond.

What was not: the ChopChop name and logo (the store name is set in Ultra instead), the original
product photography and attribute badges, and the Commerce.js branding. No code or image from
the original is included verbatim; the design was re-implemented on this codebase.
