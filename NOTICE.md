# Notice

This theme ("002 · Isle Thorne Collective") is a port of the **Isle Thorne Collective** furniture shop
template by [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/) ([codrops/isle-thorne-collective](https://github.com/codrops/isle-thorne-collective),
live demo: <https://isle-thorne-collective.crnacura.workers.dev/>), based on a design by
[Elena Smirnova](https://tympanus.net/codrops/author/elenasmirnova/), onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below:

- the visual design: the 2/12-column site grid, the type scale and palette, the comma-separated header
  navigation, the red footer bar, the home page's photo hero, collection cards with sideways photo rows
  and "All Collections" photo link, the shop index, the product page with its slideshow and fixed bottom
  bar of panels, the cart panel, the black phone menu, the newsletter panel and the red about page;
- the round arrow drawing in `components/isle.tsx` and the cart's close cross.

Product, collection and category images come from the connected store.

The font, Instrument Sans, is used under the SIL Open Font License (served by `next/font/google`).
