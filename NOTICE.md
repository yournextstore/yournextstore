# Notice

This storefront theme ports the visual design of **next-ecommerce** by buildlabs-io
(https://github.com/buildlabs-io/next-ecommerce), a Next.js + Payload CMS e-commerce starter,
onto the Your Next Store storefront. Layout, palette, header, cards, product page, cart drawer,
FAQ and footer structure follow the original; all code was rewritten for this storefront and no
original images, logos or brand names are included. Products, categories and product imagery come from
the store's own catalogue.

The footer's social and email icon paths (`lib/social-icons.ts`) come from the original's seed data
and are Font Awesome Free icons, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://fontawesome.com/license/free).
