# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of **Halfstep** by Parable (shellcat-com),
https://github.com/shellcat-com/halfstep — a single-page Vite + TypeScript landing page for a
fictional urban e-bike. Ported: the bone/graphite palette with the hi-viz lime accent and light/dark
switch, the Archivo 900 / Manrope / Space Mono type system, the floating hairline header, bracketed
mono kickers, part cards, option-card configurator, watermark numerals, the dotted layered backdrop and
the exploded-assembly scroll diagram (redrawn here as an everyday bottle; the bike drawing is not
shipped). No brand name, copy or imagery of the original ships with this theme; product data and
photography come from the store.
