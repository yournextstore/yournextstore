# Notice

This storefront theme ("Soul") is a port of the **Soul** design from
[BigCommerce Catalyst](https://github.com/bigcommerce/catalyst) (`core/vibes/soul`) onto the
Your Next Store storefront. The layout, palette (white page, near-black ink, lime accent, deep green
band), typography pairing (DM Serif Text headings, Inter body, Roboto Mono labels), pill buttons,
sticky-sidebar featured list, product carousel, slideshow, filters and footer follow the original.
No BigCommerce or Catalyst branding, logos or copy that identifies the original store is used; the
store name and every product, category and collection come from the store's own catalog.

## Images

The slideshow and about-page photographs in `public/images/pexels/` were generated for this template's store
(see "Generated photographs" below). Product photographs come from the store's catalog.
