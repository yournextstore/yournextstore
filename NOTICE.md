This theme ("018 · Fluid") is a port of the **Fluid** Hydrogen theme by Le Studio Frontvibe
([frontvibe/fluid](https://github.com/frontvibe/fluid), live demo: <https://fluid-demo.frontvibe.com>)
onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design — warm-white and espresso

near-black footer.
