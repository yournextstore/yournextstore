# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of **Emerald Garden** by Jewel Cruz
(https://github.com/jewelcruzs0922-dev/emerald-garden), released under the MIT licence.

Ported from the original: the paper-and-ink palette (paper, cream, sage, forest, terracotta), the type
pairing (Fraunces, Nunito Sans, Caveat, Caveat Brush, all SIL OFL 1.1 via Google Fonts), the torn-paper
section edges, taped polaroid, hand-cut buttons, hand-drawn line icons, product cards, page heads, buy
box and drawer layouts.

Images from the original repository used here (covered by its MIT licence):

- `public/images/landscape.webp`: the newsletter landscape illustration (`newsletter-landscape-filled.png`), re-encoded.
- `public/images/sketch-note.webp`: the taped pencil sketch (`bonsai-sketch-paper.png`), resized and re-encoded.
- `public/images/how-*.png`: the hand-drawn tree, box, van, house and leaf, cut from `how-it-works-sheet.png`
  and turned into transparent ink.

Not shipped: the original's bonsai photographs (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY / CC BY-SA, see its
`credits.txt`), its testimonial portraits, logo, brand name and copy.
