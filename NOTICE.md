# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **GoCart** by GreatStackDev
(https://github.com/GreatStackDev/gocart), released under the MIT License, onto the
Your Next Store storefront.

Ported: the gradient announcement strip, the lowercase wordmark with the green full stop,
the header (links, pill search, cart, indigo account button), the pastel hero card grid,
the category chip marquee, the centred "Latest Products" / "Featured" shelves with grey
product tiles, the "Our Specifications" cards, the pill newsletter field, the footer, the
store-info banner (used for collection and category headers), the product page layout with
Description/Reviews tabs, and the cart page's Payment Summary card (as the cart drawer).

Not ported: the multi-vendor seller dashboard, admin panel, pricing/plus membership pages and
the original's fixed demo catalogue. No images from the original repository are used: its
product photographs show third-party branded devices and its model photograph has no stated
licence. Product cards, product pages and the cart show the store's own catalogue photos; the
home hero uses a still life of the demo store's own gadgets.
