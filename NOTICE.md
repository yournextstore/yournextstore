# Notice

This theme ("015 · Contentful e-commerce template") is a port of the design of
[contentful/template-ecommerce-webapp-nextjs](https://github.com/contentful/template-ecommerce-webapp-nextjs)
(the Contentful Ecommerce Starter Template, Next.js pages router; live demo:
<https://ecommerce.templates.contentful.com>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design — Space Grotesk as the
single typeface, the Forma 36 palette (ink #1B273A, grey text #414D63, panel grey #F7F9FA, hairline
#E7EBEE, action blue #036FE3 / #0059C8), the 4px radius, the type scale (28/36px headings, 20px
titles, 14px text, 12px small caps labels), the 1280px column with a 16px gutter, the 60px header
laid transparent over the home hero, the hero's single word fitted edge to edge at -0.11em tracking
and sunk a third below the photograph, the "Trending now" tile grid (two columns, three with a wide
gutter), the product page (stacked photographs beside a grey details panel with an uppercase
"Quantity" label, a number field and a blue "Add to cart" button), "You may also like", and the
footer with its lowercase "about us".

The original has no cart, listings, menu or about page; those are built here in the same visual
language. No code, images, logos or brand names were copied: the components are written for this
storefront, the original's demo photography (served from a Contentful space, not part of the
repository) is replaced by a free Pexels photograph (below) behind the hero word and by the store's own
product images everywhere products are shown, and the "Bonelli" / "Luxette" names and
logo do not appear.
