# Notice

This storefront theme ports the visual design of **Patchbay** by Parable
(https://github.com/shellcat-com/patchbay), released under the MIT License, onto the
Your Next Store storefront.

Ported from the original: the rack-black / panel-silver palette and its light/dark token
system, the LED-amber and LED-green accents, the Space Grotesk / Inter / JetBrains Mono type
pairing, the layered "film" background, the floating header rail, the bracketed kickers, the
oscilloscope hero (canvas drawing adapted from `src/scope.ts`), the module card, spec table,
systems cards and build-slot panel layouts, and the draggable patch-cable panel with its
opt-in Web Audio voice (adapted from `src/patchbay.ts` and `src/audio.ts`, relabelled as a
generic source → level → tone → output circuit). The original ships no image files; product
imagery comes from the store's own catalogue.

Editorial photographs (the faceplates on the about page and collection headers) are generated for
this store and listed below. The "Patchbay" name and copy are not used as branding: the store name comes
from the platform.
