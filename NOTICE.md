# Notice

This storefront theme ports the visual design of **Eyesome** by Sandhya Rajwanshi
(https://github.com/SandhyaR1007/eyesome-react) onto the Your Next Store storefront: the cream
page, the Josefin Sans type with the Monoton logo face, the big "Glasses & Lens" banner, the
"Trending Products" grid, the "Categories" photo cards, the listing cards, the frosted filter
panel and the "Bag" layout.

Only the design is reused. No code was copied verbatim (the original is a React/CRA app), and none
of the original's images, product data or brand names ship with this theme: product photography,
names and prices come from the store's own catalogue, and the empty-bag illustration is drawn in
CSS/SVG.
