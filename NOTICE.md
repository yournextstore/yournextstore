# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of **Atelier Kō** by xocothemes
(https://github.com/xocothemes/atelier-ko), an Astro furniture-store theme released under the
MIT licence. The layout, design tokens (gallery-grey palette, oiled-walnut accent, hairline
structure), type scale, component classes (buttons, chips, underlined fields, quantity stepper,
spec bands) and scroll-reveal motion were adapted from it onto the Your Next Store storefront.

Not included: the Atelier Kō name and copy, and the theme's demo photography. Products,
product images, categories and collections come from the store through the Commerce Kit SDK;
editorial photographs were generated for the Holmkarve demo store (see below). Archivo
and IBM Plex Mono are loaded from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font Licence.
