# Notice

This storefront theme ports the visual design of **Blueprint**, Pack Digital's open-source
Shopify Hydrogen theme (https://github.com/packdigital/pack-hydrogen-theme-blueprint), onto the
Your Next Store storefront: its Poppins type scale, green button and promo-bar palette, header
with a full-width Shop menu, hero and 50/50 hero sections, product sliders, image tiles,
accordions, product page layout, cart drawer and black footer.

No code, images, logos or brand content from Blueprint or its demo store are shipped. Every
product and category, and the product photography, comes from the connected store through the
Commerce Kit SDK. The editorial photos are generated (see "Generated photographs" below).
