# Notice

This theme ("051 · Ecommerce") is a port of the storefront design in
[Chinmay-shete/Ecommerce](https://github.com/Chinmay-shete/Ecommerce) (live demo:
<https://ecommerce-ten-ecru-72.vercel.app>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design only — the black page with
snow type, the scrolling announcement strip, the wordmark-and-categories header with a plain
"Cart (n)", full-bleed photographic bands with bottom-left captions, full-height listing tiles that
cross-fade to a second photograph, the split product page with a ruled "Add to cart →" line, the
#1a1a1a cart drawer and the three-column footer with a ruled signup field.

Not taken: the original's brand name, its custom font (`css/font/Mihan.ttf`) and all of its
photography and video, which belong to a real brand. Type is set in Hanken Grotesk
(SIL Open Font License, via Google Fonts). Product tiles, product pages and the cart use the store's
own catalogue images; the editorial frames are generated photographs (below).
