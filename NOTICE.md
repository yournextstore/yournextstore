# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the storefront design of
**MARIUSZ E-Commerce Jewelery Store** by Piotr Borowiecki
(https://github.com/pjborowiecki/MARIUSZ-E-Commerce-Jewelery-Store), released under the MIT licence
reproduced below.

What was ported: the layout and visual language of its store front (a max-w-7xl page frame, the
uppercase wordmark with hover menus and round outline header buttons, the rounded autoplaying hero
carousel, the category and product cards, the product page with its image carousel, joined quantity
stepper and description accordion, the cart sheet, and the tinted footer with its newsletter field),
its shadcn/ui "zinc" palette and its Inter typography. It was rebuilt on the Your Next Store
storefront and its commerce SDK; no code was copied verbatim.

Not reused: the original's brand name, logo and copy, and none of its images (its product and
carousel photographs show a real client's jewellery; its avatars are photos of real people).
Product photography comes from the connected store's own catalogue; the hero carousel
photographs are AI-generated for this theme (see below).
