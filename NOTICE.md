# Notice

This storefront's design is a port of **Ledger & Vine** by Parable (shellcat-com),
https://github.com/shellcat-com/ledger-and-vine — a boutique estate-winery landing page in plain
HTML/CSS/JS. Its palette, type pairing (Cormorant Garamond, Inter, DM Mono), double ledger rules,
wine-card spec sheets, club tiers with the SVG pour, layered vineyard hero, counters and chip booking
card were rebuilt here as React components over the Your Next Store storefront.

The hill silhouettes are the original's inline SVG; the wine glass became a plain tumbler and the
copy was rewritten for Pelloure, an invented estate winery used as the demo store.
