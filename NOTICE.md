# Notice

This storefront's design is a port of **Sweet Delights** (astro-bakery) by Özgür Gökdemir
(https://github.com/ozgurgokdemir/astro-bakery), released under the MIT License. The original is a
one-page bakery site built with Astro and Tailwind CSS. Ported from it: the cream/sand palette with
burgundy and teal buttons, the Merriweather / Roboto / Pacifico type pairing and type scale, the paper
grain overlay, the fixed header whose sand backdrop fades in on scroll, the burgundy slide-in mobile
menu, the blob-masked hero photograph, the "Featured Delights" cards, the torn-paper zigzag edges
between bands, the "Welcome" band, the three-column "Pastry Portraits" gallery with its "Show more"
veil, the ruled list band, the three-column card wall, the overlapping form card and the footer.

Changes made for a real store: the listing, category, collection, product, basket and about pages are
new and extend that visual language. The original's opening hours, testimonials and contact details
were invented sample content, so they are replaced by store data: the menu's categories with their
counts, product notes drawn from the catalogue, and a menu search form.

The hero mask artwork (`public/images/hero-image-mask-*.png`) is the original's own, used under its MIT
grant. No photographs, brand name or logo from the original are shipped: its bakery photographs are
third-party stock photographs that the repository's MIT grant does not cover. Product photographs
come from the store's own catalogue, the editorial slots (hero, welcome band, "at a glance" band and
about page) use the generated photographs listed below, and the "Sweet Delights." wordmark is replaced by the store's name
in the same script face. Merriweather, Roboto and Pacifico are served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open
Font License.
