# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **Munchies** by Tinloof
(https://github.com/tinloof/munchies, demo https://munchies.tinloof.com), an Astro + Medusa +
Sanity storefront published under the MIT licence reproduced below.

What was carried over: the visual language (cream / tomato / blush palette, type scale, pill
buttons, product card with type tag, carousels, bordered text boxes, marquee, collection cards with
the drawn oval label, PDP layout, "My Bag" drawer, full-screen mobile menu, footer slab), rebuilt in
Next.js on the YNS storefront. The oval label path is taken from the original source.

What was not carried over: the Munchies name and logo, its copy, and its photography (CMS content,
not part of the repository). Products, collections, categories, product images and the store name
come from the store's own catalogue; the copy is written for a general lifestyle store.
