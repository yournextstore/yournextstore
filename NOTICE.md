# Notice

This theme's design is a port of **Next.js Commerce** by Vercel
(https://github.com/vercel/commerce): the neutral bento grid of bordered tiles with frosted
name/price pills and a blue price chip, the three-item featured grid and the marquee carousel on the home
page, the three-column listing (collections · grid · sort), the boxed product page with the arrow-pill
gallery, the frosted right-hand cart drawer and the full-screen mobile menu.

The code was rewritten onto the Your Next Store storefront (commerce-kit); no Shopify code, no
"Acme" brand name, logo or product imagery from the original is included. The logo square carries the
store's initial instead of the original mark.

Like the original, the theme has no editorial photography: every image is a product photo from the
store's own catalogue, so no stock photos are bundled.
