# Notice

This theme ("007 · Pilot") is a port of the **Pilot** storefront design from
[Weaverse/pilot](https://github.com/Weaverse/pilot) (a Shopify Hydrogen theme; live demo:
<https://pilot.weaverse.dev>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design — the Cabin / Newsreader
type pairing, the palette (ink #0F0F0F, stone #88847F, espresso #1E1C1A, sale #C6512C), the radius
scale, the scrolling announcement bar, the transparent-over-slideshow header with its centred
uppercase menu, the footer, and the compositions of the home sections (slideshow, product rail,
image banner, collection tiles, featured product, shop-the-look hotspots, promotion grid, image
gallery, icon columns, newsletter), the collection and product pages, the cart drawer and the
mobile menu.

No code, images, logos or brand names were copied: the components are written for this storefront,
the original's demo photography (served from its Shopify store, not part of the repository) is
replaced by free Pexels photographs (below) for the editorial slots and by the store's own product
images everywhere products are shown, and the "Pilot" / Weaverse names do not appear.
