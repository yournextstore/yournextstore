# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **Nordic Store** by Tailwind Toolbox
(https://github.com/tailwindtoolbox/Nordic-Store, https://www.tailwindtoolbox.com/templates/nordic-store),
released under the MIT License (reproduced below).

What was taken: the layout and visual language (Work Sans type, Tailwind grey palette, centred
bag-icon wordmark header with "Shop · About" on the left and account/cart icons on the right, the
three-slide hero carousel with its white-disc arrows, bullets and underlined "view product" link,
the uppercase "STORE" bar with icon links, the square product tiles with their hover lift, the
"ABOUT" section and the ruled footer with About / Social columns).

What was not taken: the original's photographs are third-party images hotlinked by the template and
are not covered by its MIT licence, so none are shipped. Product imagery comes from the store's own
catalogue; the hero and about photographs were generated for this store (see below). No logo or brand copy from the original is used; the store name and description come
from the store, and all other copy was rewritten. Product, listing, collection, cart and about
pages, which the single-page original does not have, extend its visual language.
