# Notice

This storefront theme ports the design of **astro-njx-verdant** by njbSaab (njX,
https://github.com/njbSaab/astro-njx-verdant), an Astro botanical-apothecary theme, onto the
Your Next Store storefront: the deep-forest palette with brass accent and light sage mode, Archivo
uppercase display type, the staggered collection tiles over the hero, product shelves, the white
services band, the editorial banner, the fullscreen menu and the floating cart card.
