# Notice

This theme ("027 · Muse") is a port of the **NJX Muse** storefront design from
[njbSaab/astro-njx-muse](https://github.com/njbSaab/astro-njx-muse) (live demo:
<https://astro-njx-muse.pages.dev>, more themes at <https://njxui.dev>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design — the ink, paper and lilac
palette, the tracked-caps type system, the masthead that sits transparent over a campaign slider with its
mega menu and slide-down search, the category tiles, double banners, arrow carousels, Shop the Look
masonry with "+" hotspots, manifesto, split newsletter band, outlined-wordmark footer, the catalogue
filter bar, product page and shopping-bag drawer, and the line icons in `app/masthead.tsx` and
`app/cart-button.tsx`.

Not taken: the original's brand name, its newsletter pop-up, its mock catalogue and product photographs.
Every product, category and price comes from the store's own catalogue.
