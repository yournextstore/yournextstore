# Notice

This theme ("004 · Medusa2 Starter") is a port of the **Barrio** coffee-roaster storefront from
[lambda-curry/medusa2-starter](https://github.com/lambda-curry/medusa2-starter) (Remix / React Router +
Medusa v2; storefront in `apps/storefront`, live demo: <https://barrio.lambdacurry.dev>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below (the repository's root `LICENSE`):

- the visual design: palette (cream, moss, olive, sand, tan), the Sen / Italiana / Ballet / Aboreto type
  pairing, header, footer, home sections (hero, "Building Community", benefits, product carousel,
  three-step band, "The Art of Roasting", community grid), collection tabs, product page, cart drawer,
  side navigation and about page compositions.

The original's café photographs and benefit icons are not used: the photographs were replaced with the
generated photographs listed below, the icons with lucide icons. The home and about section headings follow
the original's ("Building Community, one cup at a time", "The Art of Roasting", "Crafted with Care"); the
body copy is new.

Not taken: the "Barrio" name and wordmark, the original's body copy, the branded coffee-bag packshots (`header-image-*.png`), the
location photographs and addresses, and all copy naming the original brand. The wordmark and every
store name shown come from the store's own settings; product photographs come from the store's catalogue.
