# Notice

This theme ports the visual design of **Penny** by TakeShape (https://github.com/takeshape/penny),
a Next.js storefront starter released under the MIT licence: layout, type scale, palette and component
structure (header and mega menu, offers strip, split hero, product rows, category grid, product page image
grid, cart slide-over, footer). No code, images, logos or brand names from the original are shipped. Product imagery
comes from the connected store; the editorial photographs are listed below.
