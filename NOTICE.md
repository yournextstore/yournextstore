# Notice

This theme ("043 · Aspen") is a port of the storefront design of
[Weaverse/aspen](https://github.com/Weaverse/aspen) (live demo:
https://aspen-shopify-hydrogen.weaverse.dev), a Shopify Hydrogen theme, onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design (the white / warm-grey palette
with the charcoal button and deep-brown sale badge, the Tenor Sans + DM Sans pairing and the fluid
heading scale, radii; the grey announcement bar with arrows, the header with wordmark, centred uppercase
nav and mega menu, the grey mobile menu panel, the near-black footer with its giant wordmark; the home
sections: hero slideshow with progress line, "Explore collections" mosaic, shop-the-look rooms with
hotspots, featured-products swimlane, featured product with inline buy box, image-with-text, tabbed
collection band, highlight columns and customer-service accordion; the collection header with grid
switch, filter and sort, the product card, the product gallery, buy box and accordion, and the cart
drawer). The About page has no counterpart in the original and extends the same language.

Not taken: the "Aspen" and Weaverse names and logos, and the demo photography, which is Shopify store
content rather than part of the repository. The copy is rewritten for a general lifestyle store.
