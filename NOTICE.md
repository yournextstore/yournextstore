# Notice

This theme ("023 · Amerta") is a port of the storefront design of
[n-for-all/amerta](https://github.com/n-for-all/amerta), as shown on its "Kopi Luwak" demo store
(<https://demo.amerta.io>), onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design (container and grid, palette
with the orange accent, sans headings with italic-serif emphasis, announcement bar, header, footer, the
home sections: hero, image feature, orange grid showcase, product carousel, FAQ accordion, numbered
benefits, newsletter, journal cards; the listing, product and cart drawer compositions) and the shopping
bag / send icon paths.

Not taken: the demo's brand name, logo, copy and photographs. Those are CMS content of the demo store,
not part of the repository, so this theme shows the store's own product images and its own copy, with
editorial photos generated for its store (see
"Generated photographs" below). Inter and Newsreader are served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open
Font License.
