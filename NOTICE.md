# Notice

This theme ports the visual design of **Aether One** by QiwCodes
(https://github.com/qiwCodes/aether-one) onto the Your Next Store storefront: the near-black
"Obsidian" and warm-stone "Ivory" palettes and their finish switch, Cormorant and Manrope type,
the launch-stage hero with its warm key light and vignette, the hairline panels and bento grid,
the specification sheet, the centred purchase card, the brand statement and the three-part footer.

No code, images or 3D assets from the original are shipped: its react-three-fiber headphone model
is replaced by the store's own product photography lit with CSS, and all copy is rewritten for a
headphone store. The store name and catalog come from the Your Next Store API.
