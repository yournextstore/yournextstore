# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of the **Mercur storefront** ("Fleek" B2C
marketplace demo, `apps/storefront` in https://github.com/mercurjs/mercur) onto the Your Next Store
storefront. Marketplace-only features (seller pages, offers, messaging, reviews of sellers,
"sell now") were left out for a single store. No Mercur/Fleek brand name or logo is used.

The layout, type scale, palette and component styling follow the original. None of the original's
photographs, brand logos or product cut-outs are reused: the photographs in `public/images/` were
generated for this template (see "Generated photographs" below).

The typeface is Funnel Display (SIL Open Font License 1.1), loaded through `next/font/google`.
