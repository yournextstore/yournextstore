# Notice

This theme ("016 · CraveJS web template") is a port of the **Bakery Editorial** preset of the
Crave.js web storefront, [craveup-oss/cravejs-web-template](https://github.com/craveup-oss/cravejs-web-template)
(live demo: <https://cravejs-web-template.vercel.app>), onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below:

- the visual design: the warm cream palette and type scale, the editorial split hero, the browsing
  shell (category column, search, category chips, a featured row, the category index and category
  sections), the product card, the product page and quantity row, the order summary, the navigation
  drawer and the long-form footer.

The original's bakery copy and photography are not used.
