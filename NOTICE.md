# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **KARA Shop** by Mehrab (mehrabmp),
https://github.com/mehrabmp/kara-shop, released under the MIT License (text below).

What was taken over: the layout and visual language (charcoal top bar, white header with a grey
search pill and violet hover states, split hero with a violet chip and circle, the "promotions"
bento grid, white rounded product cards with round thumbnail switchers, the boxed filter sidebar,
the round pagination, the mega menu and the phone tab bar with its full-screen collections page),
re-implemented in Next.js App Router and Tailwind CSS v4 on the Your Next Store storefront.

What was not taken over: the KARA name and logo, the press/brand logos (Bazaar, Bustle, Versace,
InStyle), and all of the original's photographs (hero, promo banners, product shots). Their origin
is not documented in the repository, so this theme uses the store's own photography for products,
the hero and the featured banner.
Product, listing, cart and about pages that the original does not have were designed in its style.
