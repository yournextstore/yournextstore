# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the design of **FreshCart — Free Tailwind Ecommerce HTML Template**
by Codescandy (https://github.com/codescandy/freshcart-tailwind-ecommerce-HTML-template), free edition.

What was carried over: the layout, colour ramps (grey and green), Inter type scale, spacing, component
shapes (cards, buttons, inputs, offcanvas menu and cart) and the home page section structure. Pages the free
template does not include (product listing, category, collection, product, about) extend the same visual
language. Nothing from the paid edition was used.

Not carried over: the FreshCart name and logo, and every image from the template's repository (product,
category, banner and slider photography, avatars, payment and app-store badges). Their licences are not stated
in the repository and several show third-party brands, so product cards, the category rail, product pages
and the cart use the store's own catalogue images, and the hero slides, promo banners and the "Daily Best
Sells" tile use AI-generated photographs made for this store (below). Icons come from `lucide-react` (ISC).
