# Notice

This storefront theme ports the visual design of **Deckle** by shellcat-com / Parable
(https://github.com/shellcat-com/deckle), a single-page stationery landing built with Astro, onto the
Your Next Store storefront. Ported: the kraft / ink-blue palette and light–dark toggle, the Fraunces /
Karla / Space Mono type, the page-wide paper ruling switch (blank, dot grid, ruled with red margin,
graph), the grain and substrate layers, the open notebook with its deckled fore-edge, the
pressure-sensitive doodle pad, the range cards, the mill story layout with its index card, and the
torn till receipt (here wired to the real basket). The brand name, copy, mill facts and the
mould-and-deckle diagram are not used; product data and product images come from the store.

Editorial photographs (the notebook spreads on the home, about and collection pages) were generated
for this store; see "Generated photographs" below.
