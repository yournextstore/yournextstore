# Notice

This theme's design is a port of **CoreX Supplements**, the Hacktoberfest 2025 frontend by Open Code
Chicago (https://github.com/OpenCodeChicago/hacktoberfest-2025-frontend): the navy announcement strip,
the light header with the red-lettered wordmark and SHOP mega menu, the fading hero slider with its
navy side panel, condensed poster wordmark and script tagline, the outlined-word section titles
("Why CHOOSE …"), the navy feature band, the picture tiles with label chips, the two tilted marquee
bands, the tabbed "Best of" product carousel with white cards and navy add-to-cart bars, the blue
promo band, the "Shop by collection" cards, the dark photographic listing banners with the blue
brush-stroke headline, the boxed product page with "Highly recommended", the cart drawer and the
charcoal/midnight two-tier footer.

The code was rewritten onto the Your Next Store storefront (commerce-kit, Next.js); the original's
React/Vite/Redux code, backend and auth are not used. No CoreX brand name, logo, sub-brand logos,
product renders or photography from the original is included: product pictures come from the store's
own catalogue, the hero, tiles, cards and listing banners use AI-generated photographs made for this store,
sub-brand logos became the store's product names, and copy that made product, certification,
shipping or health claims was replaced with neutral store facts.
