# Notice

This theme ("014 · Shoppie") is a port of the **Shoppie** e-commerce home page from
[codewithsadee/shoppie](https://github.com/codewithsadee/shoppie) (an HTML/CSS/JS template; live
demo: <https://codewithsadee.github.io/shoppie/>) onto Your Next Store.

Taken from that project, under the MIT licence below: the visual design — the palette (wild-blue-yonder
lilac #A6B1D8, orange crayola #FC7840, gainsboro #D8DFDC, eerie black #121212), the type scale, the
outlined buttons with a hard 4px offset shadow, the lilac hero with its price, two-line headline and
square photograph, the scroll-snapping "Explore new arrivals" rail, the "Featured products" grid, the
offer band with its white card, the gainsboro footer with its newsletter column, and the cut-paper star,
wedge and slab shapes. The original only has a home page; the listing, collection, category, product,
cart and about pages extend that language (lilac bands, ink borders, offset shadows, orange actions).

No code, images, logos, fonts or brand names were copied: the components are written for this
storefront, the decorative shapes are redrawn as SVG, the original's model photography (no licence of
its own is stated for it) is replaced by free Pexels photographs (below) in the hero and offer band and by the store's own product images everywhere products are shown, and the "Shoppie" wordmark does not
appear. The original's display face, Clash Display (Indian Type Foundry, not covered by the MIT licence),
is replaced by Archivo (SIL Open Font License) set wide; body text is Inter (SIL Open Font License), as in
the original.
