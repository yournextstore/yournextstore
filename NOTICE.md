# Notice

This storefront's design is a port of **Petal & Stem** by shkatfrmz
(https://github.com/shkatfrmz/petal-bloom), released under the MIT License. The original is a
single-page florist shop; its palette (ink, sage, cream, paper, coral), the Playfair Display / DM Sans
pairing with coral italic accents, the announcement bar, the header with underlined search, the
sage-to-cream hero with its rotated badge, the numbered value strip, the tabbed product grid with
rounded tiles and "+ Add" pills, the cream story band, the coral newsletter band, the ink footer and
the basket drawer were ported. The listing, category, collection, product and about pages extend that
visual language.

No images, brand name or logo from the original are shipped: the "petal&stem" wordmark is replaced by
the store's own name beside a newly drawn bloom, product photographs come from the store's catalogue,
and the editorial photographs were generated for this store (the original hotlinks third-party stock
photographs, which its MIT grant does not cover). Playfair Display and
DM Sans are served by Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.
