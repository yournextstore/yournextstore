# Notice

This theme's design is a port of **Aqualux** (https://github.com/loyaltyC4/aqualux, live demo
https://aqualux-five.vercel.app), a redesign of Vercel's Next.js Commerce as a dark planted-aquarium
supplies store: the near-black "deep water" canvas with bone text and a single grow-light amber, the
amber announcement bar, the glowing-dot wordmark, the hero over a lit tank with a caustic light sweep,
the utility trust strip, the category bento, the editorial split, the snap rail, the double-bezel
product cards and newsletter band, the three-column listing with banner and price chips, the boxed
product page with the specifications panel, and the cart drawer with its progress and add-on cards.

The code was rewritten onto the Your Next Store storefront (commerce-kit). Nothing branded or
photographic from the original is included: no "Aqualux" name or mark (the wordmark is the store's
name), and none of the original's images. Its brand photographs (`public/brand/*.jpg`) carry no
licence or provenance beyond the repository's inherited code licence, and its product photos are
supplier imagery, so the editorial slots (hero, category tiles, listing banners without a photo of
their own) use the images listed below, and product cards, product pages and the cart show the
store's own product photos. The original's copy was rewritten for the demo store, a planted-aquarium
shop. Its Fontshare stylesheet (Clash Display, General Sans) is not used; the original renders in
Geist, which ships here through `next/font` (SIL Open Font License).
