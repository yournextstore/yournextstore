# Notice

This theme ports the design of **Maison**, a Shopify Hydrogen theme by Weaverse
(https://github.com/Weaverse/maison, live demo https://maison.weaverse.dev), onto the Your Next Store
storefront. Layout, palette (greige header, taupe buttons, charcoal footer), type pairing
(Belleza + Montserrat, both Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License) and section designs follow
the original. No code, images, logos or brand names from the original are shipped: its demo
photography is Shopify CMS content. Product and collection images come from the store's own
catalogue; the editorial photographs listed below were generated for this template.
The original's B2B "Become a Reseller" form is a link to the store's contact form (or the contact
block on the about page when the store has no contact form).
