# Notice

This theme ports the visual design of **Meridian** by shellcat-com / Formwork
(https://github.com/shellcat-com/formwork-meridian), a single-page espresso-equipment landing page,
onto the Your Next Store storefront: the warm near-black machined-metal palette with one burnt-copper
accent, Instrument Serif / Archivo / IBM Plex Mono type with italic copper title tails, the
full-bleed hero with its live canvas pressure gauge and shot readout, the rolling odometer
readouts, the scroll-linked copper extraction line, the machined ruler dividers, the brew-method
segmented control with its recipe card, the numbered six-step process stepper with its sticky
photo panel, the flat hairline tier cards, the care-plan strip and the footer. The original has no
shop pages; its look is extended here to the listing, collection, category, product, cart drawer,
menu and about pages.

Shipped from the original under its MIT licence: the gauge, odometer, extraction-line, tab and
stepper behaviour (ported to React). The brew-method tabs show the store's own shelves (categories)
and the six steps describe how ordering works; the gauge runs a generic test cycle. The original's
photographs and copy are not shipped (the demo store's espresso copy is written fresh); the store name and catalogue come from the Your
Next Store API, and product cards keep the store's own product photography.
