# Notice

This storefront theme ports the design of **Solace**, the Medusa starter storefront by
Rigby (https://github.com/rigby-sh/solace-medusa-starter), onto the Your Next Store
storefront and its commerce-kit SDK.

Adapted from the original: the colour palette and its tokens, the type scale, the 1328px
container and section rhythm, the header with Shop and Collections mega menus, the mobile
drill-down menu, the hero, the collection mosaic, the product carousels, the mid-page banner,
the "Get inspired" tiles, the product tile, the listing toolbar, the product page layout and
accordion, the cart dropdown, the About page and the footer, plus the line icons in
`components/icons.tsx`.

No Solace or Rigby brand name, logo or photography ships with this theme: the store's own
name, products and collection images are used instead. The original's demo photography is
served from its CMS, not included in the repository, and is not reused.
