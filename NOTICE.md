# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the storefront app of
**Pasargad / next-prisma-tailwind-ecommerce** by slowfound (formerly Sesto-Dev / Accretence),
https://github.com/slowfound/next-prisma-tailwind-ecommerce, released under the MIT License.

Ported: the shadcn/ui "neutral" look (palette, Inter type, radii), the blurred sticky header with
Products and dropdown menus, the outline search field and icon buttons, the rounded promo carousel
with white dots, the product card grid (category badge, description, price or "Out of stock",
red sale badge), the section headings and hairline separators, the right-aligned footer link
columns, the product page's image carousel beside a grey data panel with the in-cart −/count/+
stepper, and the cart page's item cards and "Receipt" card (as the cart drawer). The admin app,
auth, wishlist, blog engine and Prisma backend are not used; data comes from Your Next Store.

No images from the original or its live demo are included: the demo's banners and product photos
are third-party artwork not covered by the repository licence. Collection slides use the store's
own collection photos.
