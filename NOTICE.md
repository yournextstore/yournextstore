# Notice

This storefront theme is a port of the visual design of **Astro Ecommerce** by Creative Tim
(https://github.com/creativetimofficial/astro-ecommerce), released under the MIT License, onto the
Your Next Store storefront.

What was carried over: the layout and visual language of its landing page, product lists,
category cards, category filters, product overview, shopping cart and footer blocks (Noto Sans,
the slate palette, Bootstrap container and button metrics, card borders and shadows).

What was not: no Creative Tim or "Astro Ecommerce" branding, logos or copy, and none of the
original's images (its demo photography is not covered by the MIT License). Product
photographs come from the store's own catalogue through the Commerce API; the editorial photographs
(hero, promo bands, feature grid, newsletter band, about page) are listed below.
