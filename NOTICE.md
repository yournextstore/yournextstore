# Notice

The design of this theme is a port of **OneStopShop** by @jackblatch
(https://github.com/jackblatch/OneStopShop), used under the MIT licence below: the slate/navy
shadcn palette, the two announcement bars, the search-led header with its "Fast Dispatch" note and
dropdown menu row, the photo slideshow with a centred white card, the "Top Picks" grid, the blue
feature banner and icon cards, the bordered listing header and filter card, and the product page
layout with its feature icons and tabs.

No code, images or brand assets of the original are shipped: its marketplace features (sellers,
seller dashboard, multi-store cart) were dropped, and none of the original's hero photographs are
used. Product cards, product pages and the cart show the store's own catalogue photos; the editorial
slots use images generated for this theme's demo store (below).
