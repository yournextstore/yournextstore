# Notice

The design of this storefront theme is adapted from **React Ecommerce Boilerplate** by
Vinícius Arruda (viniarruda), https://github.com/viniarruda/react-ecommerce, used under the

What was ported: the storefront app's visual language (warm off-white surface, ink type,
"Warm Amber Gold" mustard accent, Syne display / DM Sans body / Space Mono price faces,
marquee ticker strips, the hero with its stat column, product cards, the chip-and-pill
listing toolbar, the product page layout, the categories page cards, the newsletter card
